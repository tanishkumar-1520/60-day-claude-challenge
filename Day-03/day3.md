# Day 3 - Role-Based Prompting

## What is Role-Based Prompting?

Role-Based Prompting means telling the AI to act as a specific person or expert before asking your question.

Instead of asking a normal question, you first assign a role such as a Developer, Founder, Teacher, HR Manager, or Product Manager.

This helps AI provide responses that match that role's expertise and perspective.

---

## Why Role-Based Prompting Matters

AI can answer almost any question, but when you assign a role, it understands the context better.

For example, if you ask AI to act as a Senior Software Engineer, the response will focus on technical best practices.

If you ask AI to act as a Founder, it will focus more on business strategy and growth.

---

## Example Without Role

Prompt:

How can I improve my portfolio?

Output:

Keep your portfolio clean, responsive, and include your projects.

---

## Example With Role

Prompt:

You are a Senior Frontend Developer.

Review my portfolio and suggest improvements that will help me get hired as a frontend developer.

Output:

- Improve accessibility
- Optimize loading speed
- Add GitHub live links
- Showcase responsive design
- Add performance metrics
- Improve project descriptions

---

## Three Benefits

1. Better quality responses

2. More accurate and relevant answers

3. AI behaves like a domain expert

---

## Key Learnings

Today I learned that assigning a role before asking AI improves the quality, structure, and usefulness of responses.

Role-Based Prompting helps AI think from a specific perspective, making answers more practical and professional.
