# Day 2 - Prompt Engineering

## What is Prompt Engineering?

Prompt Engineering means writing clear, specific, and detailed instructions for AI so that it can generate better and more accurate responses.

In simple words, the better your prompt, the better the AI output.

---

## Why Prompt Engineering Matters

Prompt Engineering helps AI understand exactly what you want.

When you provide proper context and instructions, AI can generate higher-quality answers, code, images, and ideas.
