# Day 51 — Product Discovery & Sprint Planning

## Project
**CareerLaunch AI**

## Challenge
AB Talks 60-Day Claude AI Challenge — Day 51

## Discovery Decision
Target audience: freshers and recent graduates seeking their first job.

## Problem
Career preparation is fragmented across resume tools, job descriptions, skill-learning resources, and interview practice.

## Product
CareerLaunch AI combines role analysis, profile review, skill-gap identification, action planning, interview practice, and progress tracking.

## v1.0
- Job description analysis
- Profile/resume review
- Skill-gap analysis
- Prioritized recommendations
- Interview practice
- Progress dashboard

## Exclusions
No automatic applications, job scraping, recruiter portal, social network, payments, or employment guarantees.

## Day 10 Success
A user completes the full preparation workflow and reaches a polished deployed v1.0.
