# CareerLaunch AI — Project Pitch Deck

## Slide 1 — Title
**CareerLaunch AI**
From job description to job-ready action plan.

## Slide 2 — Problem
Freshers face a fragmented preparation journey:
- Job requirements are difficult to interpret.
- Resume/profile weaknesses are hard to identify.
- Skill gaps are unclear.
- Interview preparation is often generic.
- Progress is difficult to track.

## Slide 3 — Target Users
**Primary:** College students and recent graduates seeking their first full-time role.

## Slide 4 — Solution
CareerLaunch AI creates one guided workflow:
**Target Role → Profile Review → Skill Gaps → Action Plan → Interview Practice → Progress**

## Slide 5 — Key Features
- Job description analysis
- Profile/resume review
- Skill-gap identification
- Prioritized recommendations
- Role-specific interview practice
- Progress dashboard

## Slide 6 — User Experience
1. Choose a target role.
2. Provide career/profile information.
3. Understand role requirements.
4. See strengths and gaps.
5. Follow prioritized actions.
6. Practice interview questions.
7. Review progress.

## Slide 7 — Technical Approach
The implementation will prioritize:
- A maintainable modular product structure
- Responsive user experience
- Clear separation of user input, analysis, recommendations, and progress
- Free/no-paid-service-first development
- Testable workflows and deployment readiness

The exact technology stack is intentionally selected during implementation rather than during product discovery.

## Slide 8 — v1.0 Boundaries
Included: preparation and readiness workflow.

Excluded:
- Automatic applications
- Job-board scraping
- Recruiter portal
- Social network
- Payments
- Employment guarantees

## Slide 9 — Future Scope
Potential post-v1.0 additions:
- More career tracks
- Saved role library
- Portfolio/project analysis
- Advanced interview simulations
- Optional integrations with job platforms
- Personalized learning resources

## Slide 10 — Vision
Make career preparation for first-time job seekers feel like a clear, guided product journey rather than a collection of disconnected tasks.

## Slide 11 — Day 10 Outcome
A polished, deployed v1.0 that a fresher can use end-to-end to understand a target role, identify gaps, create an action plan, and practice for interviews.
