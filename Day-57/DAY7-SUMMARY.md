# DAY 7 SUMMARY

## Product Refinement & User Experience

Day 7 focused on improving the capstone application's overall product experience while preserving its existing core vision and functionality.

### Product Design Review
The application was reviewed from three perspectives:

- Senior Product Designer
- UI/UX Designer
- Senior Software Engineer

The review focused on usability, visual hierarchy, consistency, responsiveness, accessibility, and regression risk.

### Refinement Areas

#### Layout
Improved visual hierarchy, alignment, spacing, and grouping of related content.

#### Typography
Reviewed heading/body hierarchy, readability, sizing, and consistency.

#### Colors
Reviewed visual consistency, contrast, emphasis, and action visibility.

#### Navigation
Reviewed the clarity of navigation and the discoverability of important application actions.

#### Responsive Design
Reviewed layouts across desktop and smaller viewport sizes to reduce overflow and improve usability.

#### States
Reviewed the application's loading, empty, and error states so users receive clear feedback during non-ideal flows.

#### Accessibility
Reviewed semantic structure, labels, keyboard focus visibility, readable contrast, and interaction clarity.

#### Micro-interactions
Reviewed transitions and feedback animations and kept them purposeful rather than decorative.

### Regression Verification
Existing functionality was re-tested after the refinement work to ensure that UI improvements did not change the intended application behavior.
