## Objective

Learned how AI can improve resume quality for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

---

## Improvements

- Improved Professional Summary
- Better ATS keywords
- Organized Skills section
- Strong action verbs
- Better project descriptions
- Reduced redundancy
- Improved readability

---

## Key Learnings

- ATS prefers simple formatting.
- Strong action verbs improve recruiter readability.
- Keywords increase ATS ranking.
- One-page resume is more effective.
- Projects should highlight impact.

Optimized my Full Stack Developer resume for ATS while keeping all information truthful.
