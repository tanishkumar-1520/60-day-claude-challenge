## 11. Important Disclaimer

Football predictions are probabilistic assessments, not guaranteed outcomes.

Historical performance, player statistics and current form can help identify patterns, but football remains unpredictable. Knockout matches can be decided by small moments, injuries, tactical decisions, penalties and individual performances.

All predictions should therefore be interpreted as evidence-based scenarios rather than certain results.
