## 🏗️ Capstone Sprint

### Day 1

**Product Discovery & Sprint Planning**

Established the product direction, requirements, priorities, and implementation roadmap.

### Day 2

**System Design**

Defined architecture, project structure, data/API considerations, and technical boundaries.

### Day 3

**Project Setup & Foundation**

Established the development foundation and repository/project workflow.

### Day 4

**Core Feature Implementation**

Moved from design into implementation of the core product functionality.

### Day 5

**Continued Feature Development**

Extended the product while reviewing existing work to preserve functionality.

### Day 6

**MVP & Working Demo**

Reached a working MVP suitable for demonstration and further refinement.

### Day 7

**Product Refinement & UX**

Improved the product from product, design, and engineering perspectives.

### Day 8

**Testing, Debugging & Production Optimization**

Reviewed functionality, quality, security considerations, and performance before launch.

### Day 9

**Launch & Production Readiness**

Prepared the application and repository for public deployment.

### Day 10

**Final Review, Portfolio & Graduation**

Completed documentation, graduation artifacts, release preparation, and Version v1.0.0.
