# 🧠 What I Learned About AI Analysis

This task demonstrated how a detailed prompt can be used to guide an AI system toward a structured domain-specific analysis.

Important learnings:

1. Providing complete input information improves the relevance of the generated response.
2. Structured prompts help AI organize complex information into sections.
3. Tables make long analytical reports easier to review.
4. Separating facts, interpretations, and probabilities makes the output more useful.
5. AI-generated predictions should be reviewed critically rather than accepted blindly.
6. Practical decisions should combine AI insights with real-world evidence.
7. Clear priorities in the prompt help the model focus on important areas such as career and wealth.
