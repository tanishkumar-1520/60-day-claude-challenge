# Day 30 – Supply Chain Builder

## Project Overview

Day 30 mein maine **Supply Chain Builder** naam ka interactive supply chain optimization simulator banaya. Is application ka purpose beginners ko supply chain ke important decisions aur unke business impact ko simple aur interactive way mein samjhana hai.

## Features

- Random company profile generation
- Supplier strategy selection
- Factory location selection
- Warehouse strategy selection
- Transportation method selection
- Inventory strategy selection
- Live business metrics
- Cost analysis
- Delivery speed analysis
- Supply chain risk analysis
- Customer satisfaction tracking
- Sustainability tracking
- Overall Supply Chain Score (0–100)
- Strengths and weaknesses analysis
- Biggest risk identification
- Three practical improvement recommendations
- Replay functionality
- Responsive dark enterprise dashboard
- Smooth transitions and animated progress bars
- Beginner-friendly explanations before every decision

## Technology Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- React 18
- React `useState`
- React via CDN
- Babel JSX

## Application Flow

1. Welcome screen par supply chain ka beginner-friendly introduction diya gaya.
2. Har playthrough mein random company profile generate hoti hai.
3. Supplier strategy select ki gayi.
4. Factory location choose ki gayi.
5. Warehouse strategy select ki gayi.
6. Transportation method choose kiya gaya.
7. Inventory strategy select ki gayi.
8. Har decision ke baad business metrics update hote hain.
9. Final optimization dashboard mein Overall Supply Chain Score calculate hota hai.
10. Strengths, weaknesses, biggest risk aur practical improvements display kiye jaate hain.
11. Replay option se different random companies ke saath simulator dobara test kiya ja sakta hai.

## Key Learning

Is project se mujhe samajh aaya ki supply chain mein ek decision ka effect business ke multiple areas par pad sakta hai.

For example, air transportation delivery speed improve kar sakta hai, lekin cost aur sustainability par negative impact ho sakta hai. Multiple suppliers disruption risk ko reduce kar sakte hain, lekin supplier management aur cost increase ho sakti hai.

Similarly, low inventory holding cost ko reduce kar sakti hai, lekin stockout risk badha sakti hai. High inventory availability improve karti hai, lekin working capital aur holding cost badh sakti hai.

## Testing

Maine simulator ko different decision combinations ke saath test kiya. Random company generation aur replay functionality ke through different business scenarios ko evaluate kiya.

Live metrics ko verify kiya gaya taaki supplier, factory, warehouse, transportation aur inventory decisions ke baad Cost, Delivery Speed, Risk, Customer Satisfaction aur Sustainability mein changes reflect hon.

## Conclusion

Supply Chain Builder ne mujhe frontend development ke saath business decision modeling, trade-off analysis, interactive dashboard design aur user-friendly simulation systems ko practically samajhne mein help ki.

Is project ka main learning ye hai ki supply chain optimization sirf cost minimize karna nahi hai. Ek effective supply chain mein cost, speed, risk, customer satisfaction aur sustainability ke beech balance banana important hai.
