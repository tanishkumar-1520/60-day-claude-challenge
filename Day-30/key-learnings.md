# Key Learnings – Day 30

* Supply chain decisions directly business performance ko affect karte hain.
* Single supplier simple hota hai but dependency risk high ho sakta hai.
* Multiple suppliers resilience improve kar sakte hain.
* Factory location cost, delivery speed aur risk ko influence karti hai.
* Warehouse strategy customer delivery aur operating cost ko affect karti hai.
* Transportation method mein speed, cost aur sustainability ke beech trade-off hota hai.
* Low inventory cost reduce kar sakti hai but stockout risk badha sakti hai.
* High inventory availability improve karti hai but holding cost badha sakti hai.
* Balanced inventory strategy different business conditions mein useful ho sakti hai.
* Live metrics user ko decisions ka immediate impact samajhne mein help karte hain.
* Randomized scenarios application ko replayable aur game-like banate hain.
* React components aur `useState` ka use karke interactive frontend application build karna seekha.
* Dashboard design mein complex business information ko simple visual metrics mein convert karna seekha.
* Good supply chain optimization ka objective sirf cost minimize karna nahi, balki cost, speed, risk, customer satisfaction aur sustainability ka balance banana hai.
