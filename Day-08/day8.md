# Day 8 – Personal Environmental Health Analyzer

## Project

Personal Environmental Health Analyzer

## Objective

The goal of this project was to create an interactive environmental health dashboard using Claude Artifacts.

The dashboard analyzes environmental data such as AQI, PM2.5, PM10 and water-quality information and presents the results through interactive charts, filters and health report cards.

---

## Features

### Key Metrics

- Average AQI
- Highest AQI City
- Lowest AQI City
- Number of Cities Analyzed
- Environmental Health Score

### Visualizations

- AQI Comparison Chart
- PM2.5 Comparison Chart
- PM10 Comparison Chart
- City Ranking Chart
- AQI Distribution Chart

### Interactive Filters

- City Selector
- AQI Range
- Pollutant Selector
- Health Risk Filter
- Date Filter
- City Comparison

### City Details

Each city includes:

- AQI
- PM2.5
- PM10
- Air Quality Category
- Health Score
- Water Quality Score

---

## Environmental Health Analysis

The dashboard provides educational information about how environmental conditions can affect areas such as:

- Respiratory health
- Sleep and energy
- Exercise conditions
- Long-term environmental exposure
- Hair and scalp care
- Skin care

Risk indicators are represented using:

- 🟢 Low
- 🟡 Moderate
- 🔴 High

These insights are intended for general educational purposes and not as medical advice.

---

## Personal Report Card

The dashboard generates:

- Air Quality Score
- Water Quality Score
- Overall Environmental Score
- Air Quality Grade
- Water Quality Grade
- Hair Risk
- Skin Risk
