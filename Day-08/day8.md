# Day 8 – Personal Environmental Health Analyzer

## Objective
Created an interactive Environmental Health Analyzer using Claude Artifacts to analyze AQI, PM2.5, PM10, water quality, and environmental health risks.

## Tool Used
Claude AI / Claude Artifacts

## What I Built
- Interactive environmental health dashboard
- AQI comparison charts
- PM2.5 and PM10 analysis
- City ranking
- AQI distribution
- Interactive filters
- City comparison
- Environmental health score
- Hair and skin risk analysis
- Personalized recommendations

## Key Features
- Average AQI
- Highest and lowest AQI city
- Cleanest and most polluted cities
- Air quality score
- Water quality score
- Overall environmental score
- Health-risk indicators
- Personalized recommendations

## What I Learned
1. How Claude can generate complete interactive dashboard artifacts.
2. How environmental datasets can be converted into visual insights.
3. How filters improve dashboard usability.
4. How data visualization makes environmental information easier to understand.
5. How AI can combine data analysis, UX design, and frontend development.

## Challenges
- Validating the generated data
- Testing interactive filters
- Checking chart behavior
- Making sure the dashboard was responsive

## Outcome
Successfully created and tested an interactive Environmental Health Analyzer using Claude Artifact.
