What is Personal AI Playbook?

It is a local, offline workspace for turning repeated AI tasks into reusable workflows. Workflow Library stores your systems. Prompt Builder assembles prompts from explained building blocks. Loop Builder adds evaluation, improvement, stop, and safety rules. Import / Export backs up your saved workflows.
