## 3. Valuation

Tickertape's retrieved snapshot showed TCS at roughly 17.4x P/E and 7.9x P/B, compared with sector figures of about 22.4x P/E and 5.8x P/B.

The skill's valuation rule requires comparison with both sector valuation and the company's own history. A complete historical valuation series was not consistently retrieved here, so a definitive cheap/fair/expensive label is withheld.

## 4. Growth

TCS reported Q1 FY27 consolidated revenue of ₹72,275 crore, up 13.9% year-on-year and 2.2% sequentially in INR. Operating margin was 24.0%, net margin 19.2%, and TCV was US$9.5 billion.

Annualized AI revenue reached US$2.6 billion, up 13.6% sequentially. The quarter included an US$800 million mega deal with SKF plus other AI-led transformation agreements.

## 6. Financial Health

### Debt

Debt/equity is approximately 0.10 in the retrieved Tickertape snapshot.

Using the skill's rules:

- <1 = Safe
- 1–2 = Moderate
- >2 = Leveraged

TCS falls in the Safe category on this metric.

### Cash Flow

TCS's FY2026 annual report reports operating cash flow of ₹52,094 crore versus ₹48,908 crore in FY2025.

A separate financial-data source reports FY2026 free cash flow of approximately ₹47,870 crore, up 6.55% year-on-year.

Cash generation is therefore a major fundamental strength.

## 12. Latest News / Governance Risk

In August 2026, N. Chandrasekaran announced that he would step down as Tata Sons chairman when his current term ends in February 2027 after insufficient board support for another term.

Reuters reported that TCS was among the Tata companies affected by the market reaction, with TCS falling sharply on 12 August.

This is mainly a governance/sentiment risk and is not, by itself, evidence of a deterioration in TCS operating fundamentals.
